Account questions

Can I change my account type later?

Yes. Open a second account of a different type from your client portal, or ask support to convert your account when you have no open positions.

Is there an inactivity fee?

No. Valora Markets does not charge a fee for inactive accounts.

Do you offer swap-free accounts?

Swap-free trading is available on Standard and Raw accounts for clients who need it. Contact support to apply.
