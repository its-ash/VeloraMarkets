Ask our team
Why do you only offer nTrader?

One platform lets us tune pricing, execution and support for one stack. Every feature we ship works on desktop, web and mobile at the same time.

How long does account approval take?

Most applications are verified the same business day after you upload a valid ID and proof of address.

Are client funds kept separate?

Yes. Client money is held in segregated accounts at tier-one banks and is never used for company operations.

Can I practise before I fund an account?

Open a free demo on nTrader with virtual funds and live prices. You can switch to a live account at any time.

What are the withdrawal fees?

Valora Markets does not charge withdrawal fees. Your bank or payment provider may apply its own charges.
